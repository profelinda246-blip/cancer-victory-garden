Free Floral Arranging Classes

Experience the beauty and relaxation of fresh flowers

The Cancer Victory Garden hosts free flower arranging classes at Hearst Cancer Resource Center monthly. There is no charge to survivors and we provide all the flowers we grow in our garden.
