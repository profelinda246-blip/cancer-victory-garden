San Luis Obispo

A Garden Sanctuary of Growth and Support for Cancer Patients

Welcome to a San Luis Obispo garden sanctuary that embodies hope and growth. We unite survivors, caregivers, and the steadfast backing of loved ones in a vibrant setting. In partnership with esteemed institutions, we offer solace and inspiration to cancer patients and survivors.

Our survivor volunteers plant flower seeds, grow them to maturity in our garden, cut the flowers, arrange them into bouquets in donated vases, and then distribute them to our cancer related charities, all the while enjoying the support and camaraderie of fellow cancer patients, survivors and volunteers.

In partnership with
Hearst Cancer Resource Center — Dignity Health Dignity Infusion Center
Our Mission

Volunteer, Heal, and Share Love through Gardening & Flowers

We, as cancer survivors, come together to support and inspire others in their cancer journeys through gardening. The garden serves as a healing sanctuary where patients find hope amid vibrant blooms.

Our mission is to empower survivors to volunteer in the garden, spreading hope throughout our community. Flower arranging classes led by survivors and volunteers create beautiful arrangements filled with love for cancer patients. This selfless act fosters support, personal growth, and an appreciation for life's fragility.

In this space of resilience and compassion, we share love, touch lives, and make a meaningful impact on individuals and communities.
